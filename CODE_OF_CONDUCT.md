# Code of conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community for everyone.

Be constructive, assume good faith, respect privacy, and keep discussions focused on authorized and lawful security work. Harassment, discrimination, doxxing, threats, spam, and instructions intended primarily for abuse are not acceptable.

Repository maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community. Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's contact channel or GitHub's reporting tools.
